About

Hiii, my name is Tiffany! I am a second year Digital Media Arts (DMA) major attending San Jose State University. I primarily work digitally using programs such as Procreate, Photoshop, and Lightroom Classic. I hope to one day work as a concept artist for video games as it aligns with my interests (gaming and working digitally). I also hope to one day inspire others in their pursuit of art. My passion for art stems from the inspiration that I get from other artists! One principle that I have been trying to apply to my process in art is “not thinking and just doing.” I think a lot of the time artists (including myself) get scared to create something “ugly” and in turn end up creating less or not creating at all. Rather than staying in your comfort zone, jump into the unknown and continue to do something that you don’t enjoy until you do. Don’t dwell on trying to make every outcome perfect, you won’t learn anything that way :). A few random personal fun facts about me unrelated to art: I REALLY REALLY love savory foods (specifically charcuterie boards and steak), I enjoy go kart racing heh, anddd I like to watch 3FS on YouTube. Please stick around to see me grow as an artist!!!
